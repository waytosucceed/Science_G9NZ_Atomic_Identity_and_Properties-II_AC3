Short Answer Questions

Q1
If chlorine atom is available in the form of two isotopes:

Calculate the average atomic mass of the bromine atom.

The average atomic mass of chlorine is calculated using the weighted average of its isotopes:

Average atomic mass =
(35 x 75/100) + (37 x 25/100)
= (105/4) + (37/4)
= 142/4
= 35.5 u

So, the average atomic mass of chlorine is approximately 35.5 u.
